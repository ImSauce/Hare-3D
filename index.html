Tips for AR
  1. Allow camera access when asked.
  2. Point at the floor and move your phone slowly side to side until she appears.
  3. Drag to move her, pinch to resize, and twist with two fingers to turn her.
  4. Animations loop, so the "…loop" ones look the smoothest.
